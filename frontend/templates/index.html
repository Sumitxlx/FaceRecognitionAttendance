HOW IT WORKS

From camera to attendance

01

Enroll

A student's face is captured and processed through quality checks and alignment.

02

Recognize

The live face is converted into a numerical representation and compared with enrolled students.

03

Mark Attendance

A sufficiently confident recognition can create an attendance record for the active session.

04

Analyze

Attendance records can be used for dashboards, trends and predictive defaulter analytics.
